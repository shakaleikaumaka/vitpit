The Knowledge Transponder

Press play. The words glow.

Every session opens the same way 🌊 — a tiny window plays the talk in the corner, small on purpose (alpine wifi forgives small pictures), the alpine night breathes behind it, and below, the transcript glows paragraph by paragraph as it's spoken — calm, no chasing. 🕳️ Why the P.I.T.?

Public Information Transmission

P.I.T. is the Knowledge Transponder's true backronym — and it's named twice over. For the orchestra pit, where the musicians sit: the ones who make the sound the whole room dances to, rarely in the spotlight, always holding the signal. And in honor of OSO P.I.T. — Shaka's ETHGlobal Delhi hackathon winner (Most Creative Use of ENS), where the pit first got its name.

publicinform.com 🕳️✨

And the pit is only just beginning — onward, sharing this openly with communities everywhere, for all: the open transponding of knowledge. The pit provides! 💪🕳️

This pit is a fork. 🍴

In May 2025, ~200 builders gathered in the Swiss Alps for Zuitzerland — a network-state sandbox prototyping the civilization of tomorrow. Shaka helped the gathering and ran the A/V. The sessions shouldn't vanish — so the pit forked.

This is the first fork of The Esmeralda P.I.T. — the genesis archive where the protocol was proven (149 sessions · 122 hours · 138 synced transcripts · ≈$0). Same DNA, alpine blood: the Knowledge Transponder, the radical honesty, the ≈$0 static architecture.

Everything here is open. Watch it, read it, quote it, teach with it, remix it into your own sandbox. The knowledge belongs to the commons — open source the knowledge, not just the music.

And if you want to build with it — the whole pit is on GitHub: this site, the data, the transcripts, and the pipeline that harvested them. Everything open-sourced CC0. Fork me like crazy. 🍴
